# 🏥 Health & Vaccination Details Page - Navigation Guide

## ✅ HOW TO ACCESS THE HEALTH RECORD DETAILS PAGE:

### **Method 1: From Overview Tab (Recommended)**
1. Click on **"Health & Vaccination"** in the sidebar
2. You'll be on the **"Overview"** tab by default
3. Scroll down to **"Recent Health Records"** section
4. You'll see **4 health record cards** displayed in a grid
5. **Click on ANY card** to view full details
6. ✨ **The detail page will open!**

### **Method 2: From Health Records Tab**
1. Click on **"Health & Vaccination"** in the sidebar
2. Click the **"Health Records"** tab at the top
3. You'll see a **list of ALL health records**
4. Use the **search bar** or **filters** to find specific records
5. **Click on ANY record card** to view full details
6. ✨ **The detail page will open!**

## 🎯 DETAIL PAGE FEATURES:

### **Top Actions:**
- 🖨️ **Print** - Print the health record
- 📥 **Download** - Download as PDF
- 📤 **Share** - Share via Email/WhatsApp/PDF
- ✏️ **Edit** - Edit the record
- 🗑️ **Delete** - Delete with confirmation

### **Tabs Available:**
1. **Overview** - Vital signs, Symptoms, Diagnosis, Vaccination, Notes
2. **Treatment** - Medicines, Procedures, Care instructions
3. **History** - Medical history timeline

### **Information Sections:**
- ✅ Animal Information (Tag, Name, Breed)
- ✅ Farmer Information (Name, ID)
- ✅ Veterinary Officer (Name, License, Contact)
- ✅ Vital Signs (Temperature, Heart Rate, Respiratory Rate)
- ✅ Symptoms & Diagnosis
- ✅ Treatment Details
- ✅ Vaccination Info (if applicable)
- ✅ Follow-up Alerts
- ✅ Cost Breakdown
- ✅ Notes & Attachments
