## 📋 **AVAILABLE DISEASE ALERTS**

### **Alert 1: Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD)**
- **Severity:** 🔴 HIGH
- **Affected Areas:** 3 blocks (Brahmagiri, Krushnaprasad, Satyabadi)
- **Cases:** 47 animals
- **Trend:** 📈 Increasing
- **Last Updated:** 2 hours ago

**Symptoms Include:**
- Round nodules on skin (2-5 cm)
- High fever (40-41°C)
- Reduced milk production
- Loss of appetite
- Nasal and eye discharge
- Swelling in legs
- Depression and weakness

**Vaccination:** Available (Single dose + annual booster)

### **Alert 2: Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD)**
- **Severity:** 🟡 MEDIUM
- **Affected Areas:** 1 block (Puri)
- **Cases:** 12 animals
- **Trend:** → Stable
- **Last Updated:** 1 day ago

**Symptoms Include:**
- Blisters in mouth, tongue, feet
- High fever (104-105°F)
- Excessive salivation
- Lameness
- Loss of appetite
- Reduced milk production
- Difficulty eating

**Vaccination:** Available (Every 6 months - Trivalent)
